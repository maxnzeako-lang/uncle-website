What is PSMF About?

Professor Sidney’s Mentorship Forum (PSMF) bridges theoretical biological knowledge and applied research, empowering students with critical analytical and field capabilities.

Research Methodology

Training students in rigorous experimental design, sampling techniques in parasitology, soil nematology, and eco-toxicology.

Academic Publishing

Guiding young scholars in writing peer-reviewed journal papers, thesis defense preparation, and literature analysis.

Grant & Career Advisory

Advisory for research grant applications, fellowship nominations (TETFund, IFS), and international scholarship applications.

Academic Biography

Professor Sidney is a distinguished academic and environmental biologist at the Department of Animal and Environmental Biology, University of Port Harcourt. He specializes in Nematology, Parasitology, Ecotoxicology, and Invertebrate Zoology.

His work focuses on environmental pollution assessment in Niger Delta aquatic ecosystems, soil nematode population metrics, and vector control strategies for tropical parasites. Through PSMF, he provides continuous academic mentorship for university students.
